How hunts are scored

  • Rut phase uses Delta-region timing: peak breeding Dec 26 – Jan 8, chase phase Dec 10 – Dec 25.
  • Moon favors the new moon (dark nights push daylight feeding), with a smaller bump near the full moon for midday movement.
  • Multi-day hunts are scored on their best day, not the opener.
  • Permits is a draw-odds proxy — more permits offered means a better chance of being drawn.

Drive times are estimates from a road-distance model — confirm routes before you commit.
