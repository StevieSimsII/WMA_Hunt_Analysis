# Theodore Roosevelt NWR Complex — 2026 lottery deer hunts

Source: TR Complex online lottery entry cards (USFWS). Separate application
system from MDWFP WMA draws — entering these does NOT use up any of the five
MDWFP deer choices.

All TR Complex hunts require the **TR Complex Annual Public Use Permit**
(FREE Senior version for the senior hunt). Hunters must possess a MS hunting
license, stamps, and permit, or be exempt.

Buck restriction across all three: **15" inside spread or 18" main beam**.
Daily bag: one buck + one antlerless. Hunt bag: one buck + two antlerless.

Quotas are not published on the entry cards.
